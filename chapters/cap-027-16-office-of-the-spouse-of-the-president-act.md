*11 of 2009*

An Act to make provisions for the establishment of a public office for the spouse of the President.

### [1 ST JANUARY, 2012]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Office of the Spouse of the President Act.

*Office of the Spouse of the President.*

**2.** (1) There shall be a public office called the Office of the Spouse of the President:

Provided that where the office is held by a woman, it shall be called the Office of the First Lady.

(2) The Office of the Spouse of the President shall be held by the spouse of a serving President and its tenure shall be co- terminus with that of the serving President.

*Entitlement to allowances and other benefits for discharging public functions.*

**3.** The allowances and other benefits to which the holder of the Office of the Spouse of the President, in discharging public functions, is entitled to may be specified by the Minister.
