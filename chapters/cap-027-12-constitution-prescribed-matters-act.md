*21 of 1967*

An Act to prescribe the age for the vacation of office by the holders of certain offices as authorised by the Constitution of Guyana.

[20 th SEPTEMBER 1967]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Constitution (Prescribed Matters) Act.

*Age for vacation of office by the holders of certain offices. [4 of 1988]*

**2.** The Director of Audit and the Commissioner of Police shall vacate their respective offices on attaining the age of sixty years and fifty-five years respectively:

Provided that—

(a) the President, acting on the recommendation of the Public Service Commission, may permit a Director of Audit who has attained the age of sixty years to continue in office until he has attained such later age, not exceeding sixty-five years, as may (before the Director of Audit has attained the age of sixty years) have been agreed with the Director;

(b) the President, acting on the recommendation of the Police Service Commission may permit a Commissioner of Police who has attained the age of fifty-five years to continue in office until he has attained such later age, not exceeding sixty years, as may (before the Commissioner of Police has attained the age of fifty-five years) have been agreed with the Commissioner of Police;

(c) any continuance in office effected pursuant to paragraph (a) or (b) may from time to time be extended in like manner as herein before provided but not beyond the age of sixty-five years in the case of the Auditor General and sixty years in the case of the Commissioner of Police.
