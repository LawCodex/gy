## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

*SECTION Short*

**1.** title.

**2.** Surveys by aerial photography.

__________________________

*29 of 1970*

An Act to authorise the surveying of lands by means of aerial photography.

[ 12 th DECMEBER, 1970]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Surveys (Special Provisions) Act.

*Surveys by aerial photography.*

*c. 5:02*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any other law, a plan or diagram may, either in conjunction with, or without the procedures prescribed by the Land Surveyors Act, be prepared in accordance with a survey carried out to the satisfaction of the Commissioner of Lands by means of low altitude aerial photography, demonstrating for the purposes of the award of titles under the Land Registry Act, or of any conveyance in respect of any State or Government lands, the situation, boundaries and relative position of land in a land registration area declared under the Land Registry Act or of any such State or Government lands and any plan or diagram so prepared shall for all purposes have effect as if it had been prepared under the Land Surveyors Act.
