*40 of 1903*

An Act for the Prevention of Fraudulent Dealings with Balata and substances of the like nature.

### [14 TH NOVEMBER, 1903]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Balata Act.

*Interpretation. [9 of 1977]*

**2.** In this Act—

“balata” includes rubber and any substance of a like nature;

*c. 67:01*

“officer” includes any forest officer under the Forests Act, any district commissioner and any member of the police

*force.*

*No sale of out a balata without a licence.*

**3.** (1) No one shall sell or export balata unless he takes licence for so doing.

(2) The licence shall not be issued to anyone

*unless—*

(a) he is the holder of a licence for obtaining balata, issued under the State Lands Regulations for the time being in force;

(b) he satisfies a district commissioner that he is the owner or lawful occupier of land on which bullet trees are growing; or

(c) he is the duly authorised agent of the persons aforesaid.

*issue any*

(3) A district commissioner shall not be bound to licence under this section.

*No purchase of balata without a licence a licence.*

**4.** (1) No one shall purchase balata unless he takes out for so doing.

*issue a*

(2) A district commissioner shall not be bound to licence under this section.

*Licensees to keep books. balata [6 of 1997]*

**5.** (1) Everyone licensed to sell, export, or purchase shall, keep a book in which shall be entered—

(a) the name and address of the person to whom he sells, or from whom he purchases it, or for whom as agent he sells or exports it;

(b) the quantity of balata so sold, purchased, or exported;

(c) the price at which it was sold and purchased;

(d) the date of the sale, purchase, or export; and

(e) if he is the seller or exporter thereof, the place from which it was obtained.

(2) The book shall be open at all reasonable times to the inspection of any officer.

(3) Anyone licensed as aforesaid who neglects to keep the book, or duly to enter therein the particulars hereinbefore required, or who refuses to allow it to be inspected by an officer, shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of nineteen thousand five hundred dollars.

*Licence issued to owner of private land to specify quantity of balata to be sold under it. [6 of 1997]*

**6.** (1) Whenever a licence is issued under section 3 to anyone who is the owner or lawful occupier of land on which bullet trees are growing, the licence shall specify on the face thereof the quantity of balata which the person licensed may sell or export under it, and anyone so licensed who during the currency of his licence sells or exports a greater quantity of balata than that specified in his licence, shall be liable to a fine of nineteen thousand five hundred dollars.

(2) The quantity of balata to be specified in a licence as the quantity which may be sold or exported thereunder shall be the quantity which the district commissioner issuing the licence considers may reasonably be expected to be collected from the land owned or occupied by the person so to be licensed during the period for which the licence is to be issued, and for the purpose of estimating that

quantity, the district commissioner, before issuing the licence, may require that land and the bullet trees upon it to be inspected by an officer at the expense of the applicant for the licence.

*Issue of licences. [6 of 1997]*

**7.** (1) Licences to sell, export, or purchase balata may be issued by the district commissioner.

*c. 80:01*

(2) A licence shall expire on the 31st March next after its issue, and the person to whom it is issued shall pay for it the sum fixed by the Tax Act for the time being in force.

*Penalty for selling without.*

(3) Everyone who sells, exports, or purchases balata without having a licence in force for so doing under this Act shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of forty-eight thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars.

*Imported balata excepted from Act.*

**8.** This Act shall not apply to any balata imported into Guyana from any country or place outside Guyana.

*Prosecution of offences.*

**9.** Any offence created or penalty imposed by this Act may be prosecuted or recovered under the Summary Jurisdiction Acts.
