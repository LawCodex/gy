*12 of 1992*

An Act to provide for the granting of pensions and gratuities to officers and other ranks of the Guyana National Service.

### [1 ST OCTOBER 1974]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Guyana National Service (Pensions and Gratuities) Act.

*Interpretation.*

*c. 1:03*

**2.** In this Act “Guyana National Service” has the same meaning as in section 2 of the Representation of the People Act.

*Application of the Defence (Pension and Gratuities) Regulations to the Guyana National Service. c. 15:01*

**3.** (1) The provisions of the Defence (Pensions and Gratuities) Regulations shall apply in relation to an officer or other rank of the Guyana National Service as they apply in relation to an officer or soldier of the Guyana Defence Force and for that purpose shall be construed with any necessary modifications, adaptations, qualifications and exceptions, and, without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, subject, in particular for the purpose of such application, to the provisions of subsections (2) and (3).

(2) Reference in the said Regulations to officers

and soldiers shall be construed as being references to officers and other ranks of the Guyana National Service; and references to Force shall be construed as references to the Guyana National Service.

(3) Regulation 20(2) shall be construed and have effect as if there were inserted the following paragraph immediately after paragraph (f)—

“(ff) service in the Guyana Defence Force;”
