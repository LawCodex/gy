### CHAPTER 27:15 PRESIDENT HOYTE PENSIONS ACT

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

*SECTION Short*

**1.** title.

**2.** Widow’s pension.

*8 of 2009*

__________________________ An Act to make special provisions relating to the pension of the late President Hoyte.

### [22 ND DECEMBER, 2002]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the President Hoyte Pensions Act.

*Widow’s pension. c. 27:03*

*c. 27:13*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything in section 5 of the Pensions (President, Parliamentary and Special Offices) Act, the widow of the late President Hoyte shall be entitled to receive an enhanced pension calculated in accordance with section 3 of the Pensions (President) Act from the date when the widow's pension first became payable.
