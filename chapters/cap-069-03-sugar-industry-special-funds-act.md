*20 of*

1947 An Act to make provision for the establishment in respect of the Sugar Industry of a Price Stabilisation Fund, a Rehabilitation Fund and a Labour Welfare Fund and for purposes connected therewith.

[1 st JANUARY, 1947]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Sugar Industry Special Funds Act.

**2.** For the purposes of this Act there is established by Establishment

*of*

special funds. the Secretary to the Treasury in the accounts of Guyana the following special funds—

(a) a Sugar Industry Price Stabilisation Fund;

(b) a Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund; and

(c) a Sugar Industry Labour Welfare Fund.

*Secretary to the Treasury to be trustee of special funds.*

**3.** Subject to this Act, the Secretary to the Treasury is trustee of the special funds specified in section 2.

*Investment of special funds and exemption of trustee from payment of income tax.*

**4.** (1) The Secretary to the Treasury may, with the approval of the Minister, invest or place on deposit account any of the moneys of any of the special funds specified in section 2, and any interest earned on moneys so invested or deposited shall be placed to the credit of the appropriate special fund.

*c. 81:01*

(2) The Secretary to the Treasury, in his capacity as trustee of the special funds specified in section 2, is exempt from the payment of income tax under the Income Tax Act.

*Amount to be levied on sugar exporters and paid to Comptroller of Customs. [23 of 1974 O. 43/1992 O. 2/1993 O. 33/1993]*

**5.** (1) Subject to section 7, every exporter of sugar shall pay to the Comptroller of Customs an amount which shall be levied at the rate of five hundred and fourteen dollars and fifty cents on every ton of sugar, manufactured in Guyana on and after 1st January, 1993, which is exported therefrom.

(2) The amount to be paid by an exporter of sugar under subsection (1) shall be levied and collected within ninety days after the date of exportation.

(3) In default of payment when due of any amount payable under this section by an exporter of sugar, such amount, together with interest at the rate of six per cent per

annum from the date the payment became due under subsection (2) shall, after demand for payment has been made by the Comptroller of Customs, be recovered by the Secretary to the Treasury against the exporter by parate execution.

*Allocation to special funds of amounts paid under section 5. [23 of 1974 17 of 1991 O. 33/1993]*

**6.** The Comptroller of Customs shall pay to the Secretary to the Treasury the amounts paid to him under section 5, and the Secretary to the Treasury, subject to section 7, shall allocate such amounts to the credit of the special funds specified in section 2 in the proportions hereinafter set out, that is to say—

(a) to the Sugar Industry Price Stabilisation Fund, a proportion equivalent to two dollars and fifty cents on every ton of sugar manufactured and exported;

(b) to the Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund, a proportion equivalent to twelve dollars on every ton of sugar manufactured and exported; and

(c) to the Sugar Industry Labour Welfare Fund, a proportion equivalent to five hundred dollars on every ton of sugar manufactured and exported:

(a) at any time and from time to time vary the rate specified in section 5(1);

(b) at any time and from time to time vary the proportions set out in section 6;

*Committees to manage the special funds. [20 of 1951*

**8.** (1) The Sugar Industry Price Stabilisation Fund and the Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund shall be controlled and administered by a Committee to be appointed by the

Minister, and the Committee shall consist of the following members—

(a) four persons nominated by the Guyana Sugar Corporation Limited;

(b) one person who is a farmer within the meaning of the National Cane Farming Committee Act;

(c) not less than two but not more than three persons nominated collectively by the trade unions concerned in the welfare of labour workers in the sugar industry; and

(d) two public officers.

(2) The Sugar Industry Labour Welfare Fund shall be controlled and administered by a Committee which shall be a body corporate to be appointed by the Minister, and the Committee shall consist of the following members—

(3) The seal of the Committee appointed under subsection (2) shall be authenticated by the signatures of the Chairman and the Secretary of the Committee or in such other manner as may be authorised by resolution of the Committee and every document purporting to be an instrument duly executed under the seal of the Committee shall be received in evidence and deemed, without further proof, to be so executed unless the contrary is proved.

(4) All documents, other than those required by law to be under seal, made by, and all decisions of, the Committee appointed under subsection (2) may be signified under the hand of the Chairman of the Committee or the Secretary thereof, or by any person authorised by resolution of the Committee.

*Regulations.*

**9.** (1) The Committee appointed under section 8(1), and the Committee appointed under section 8(2) may, with the approval of the Minister, make regulations to provide for the following matters—

(a) the basis and the conditions on which disbursements from the appropriate special funds shall be allowed and authorised, and the procedure which shall govern the making of such disbursements;

(b) the keeping of proper records, and the keeping and rendering of detailed accounts, in relation to the appropriate special fund, and in particular the keeping and rendering of detailed accounts in respect of the portion of the special fund which relates to each exporter of sugar.

(2) The Minister may, with respect to matters not

specifically mentioned in subsection (1), make regulations and prescribe forms for carrying into effect the purposes of this Act, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing provision regulations may be made providing for the following matters—

(a) the term of office of a member of a Committee, and the circumstances under which such office shall become vacant;

(b) the appointment of a Chairman of a Committee;

(c) the grant of leave of absence to a member of a Committee, and the appointment of another person during such leave;

(d) the quorum required to be present for the transaction of business by a Committee.

### SUGAR INDUSTRY (REHABILITATION FUND)

### REGULATIONS

made under section 9(1)

*Citation.*

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Sugar Industry (Rehabilitation Fund) Regulations.

*Interpretation. [reg. 8/1956] “the*

**2.** In these Regulations –

Committee" means the Committee appointed under section 8 (1) of the Act to control and administer the Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund established under the Act;

“exporter" means any person operating a factory in Guyana and manufacturing sugar for export;

*“the*

Fund" means the Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund established under the Act;

“rehabilitation" means the extension, renovation or reconstruction of any existing factory or ancillary building pertaining thereto, or the replacements or improvements to any existing plant or machinery in a factory, or the installation of any new plant, machinery, or engine in a factory for the purpose of increasing efficiency in production or for safeguarding any existing factory, but does not include normal maintenance;

*“the*

*Reg. 28/1947*

Secretary" means the person appointed to discharge the duties of Secretary to the Committee under regulation 11(1) of the Sugar Industry (Rehabilitation and Price Stabilisation Committee) Regulations.

*Registration of exporters.*

**3.** The Secretary shall keep a register of exporters, and shall register therein every exporter who applies to be registered with the Committee together with the factories operated by him as hereinafter provided.

**4.** Any exporter may apply in writing to be registered with the Committee, and every such exporter shall specify in his application the sugar factories operated by him.

**5.** The Secretary shall issue a certificate of registration to every exporter registered by him as aforesaid.

**6.** Every registered exporter shall notify the Committee in writing whenever any factory belonging to him changes ownership or ceases to manufacture sugar for export.

*Quarterly statements to be submitted by registered exporters.*

**7.** Every registered exporter shall, before the 31st January, the 30th April, the 31st July, and the 31st October of each year, submit to the Committee certified statements of all sugar exported by him during the preceding three months.

*Duties of Secretary.*

**8.** The Secretary shall keep a register showing –

(a) the quantity of sugar exported from time to time by each exporter;

(b) the amount of the levy to be allocated to the Fund in respect of each exporter in accordance with section 6(b) of the Act;

(c) the date when payment of such levy is due; and

(d) the date when payment of such levy is made.

**9.** The Secretary shall keep such books of account and registers as are necessary to render detailed accounts in respect of such portions of the Fund as relate to each exporter.

**10.** The Secretary shall keep a register of all claims made upon the Fund as hereinafter provided, and shall record therein the decisions of the Committee in respect of every such claim.

**11.** The Secretary shall, as soon as possible after the 31st December of each year, prepare and lay before the Committee preparatory to submission to the Minister a report upon the administration of the Fund during the preceding year, together with a Balance Sheet and Statement of Revenue and Expenditure.

**12.** Copies of the annual reports and statements shall be forwarded by the Secretary to the Sugar Producers Association for distribution to each registered exporter.

*Inspection of books of account.*

**13.** All books of account, registers, and documents relating to the Fund shall be at all times open to inspection by any member of the Committee.

*Allocation of interest.*

**14.** Any interest earned on any moneys invested or deposited by the Secretary to the Treasury under the Act shall be allocated once in every year to such portions of the Fund relating to the various exporters in such proportions as the Committee may deem fit.

*Annual audit.*

**15.** The accounts of the Committee shall be audited once in every year by the Director of Audit, and the Committee shall pay therefor such fee as may be approved by the Minister.

*Employees.*

**16.** The Committee may employ such persons as they may think necessary, in addition to the Secretary, at such

salaries as they may think fit.

*Expenses.*

**17.** All expenses arising out of the control and administration of the Fund shall be borne by the Fund, and shall be apportioned and charged to the Fund in any manner the Committee may think fit.

*Claims which may be made on the Fund.*

**18.** (1) Any registered exporter may claim to be reimbursed from the Fund for any work of rehabilitation carried out by him, or to be advanced any sum of money to defray the cost of any work of rehabilitation contemplated by him.

(2) Any registered exporter making any claims as aforesaid shall declare in writing to the Committee –

(a) the amount claimed;

(b) the nature of the work of rehabilitation already carried out or in process of being carried out or contemplated; and

(c) the actual or estimated cost, as the case may be.

(3) Every claim for reimbursement shall be accompanied by a certified statement as to the amount expended, and that such sum was utilised solely on the work mentioned in the claim.

(4) Any registered exporter to whom payment of any sum of money from the Fund has been made under these Regulations, shall within six months of the date of the completion of the work of rehabilitation in respect of which such payment has been made, submit to the Committee a statement certified by an auditor or such other person as may

be approved by the Committee, of the expenditure incurred in respect of such work of rehabilitation.

*Power of the Committee with regard to claims.*

**19.** The Committee may, in their discretion, grant or refuse any claim made under the last preceding regulation or may authorise the payment of any smaller sum.

*Power to enter and inspect factories in respect of which claims have been made.*

**20.** The Committee or any member thereof, or any person appointed by the Committee may, upon due notice being given, enter and inspect any factory in respect of which a claim has been made on the Fund.

*Method of payment of disbursements and claims.*

**21.** All disbursements and payments shall be made by the Secretary to the Treasury upon presentation of the relevant voucher, duly certified by the Chairman of the Committee and the Secretary.

*Reg. 28/1947*

### SUGAR INDUSTRY (REHABILITATION AND PRICE STABILISATION COMMITTEE) REGULATIONS

made under section 9(2)

*Citation.*

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Sugar Industry (Re-habitation and Price Stabilisation Committee) Regulations.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In these Regulations "the Committee" means the Committee appointed under section 8(1) of the Act to control and administer the Sugar Industry Price Stabilisation Fund and the Sugar Industry Rehabilitation Fund established under the Act.

*Term of office of Committee.*

**3.** The Committee shall remain in office for the term of two years from the date of its appointment and shall in the manner provided by the Act be re-appointed every two years.

*Appointment of Chairman.*

**4.** On the appointment of a Committee, and whenever there is a vacancy in the office of Chairman, the Minister shall appoint a member of the Committee to be Chairman.

*Quorum at meetings of Committee.*

**5.** (1) Three members present at any meeting of the Committee shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

(2) The Chairman shall preside at all meetings of

*[Subsidiary] Sugar*

Industry (Re-habitation and Price Stabilisation Committee) Regulations

the Committee which he attends, and in his absence the members present may elect one of their number to preside over the meeting.

*Questions determined by majority of votes and Chairman to have casting vote.*

**6.** Every question before the Committee shall be determined by the votes of a majority of the members present, and the Chairman or other presiding member shall have an original vote thereon and also a casting vote if the votes are equally divided.

*Leave of absence.*

**7.** (1) The Minister may grant leave of absence to any member of the Committee.

(2) Where leave has been granted the Minister may, subject to section 8 (1) of the Act, appoint another person to act in the place of the member of the Committee who has been granted such leave.

*Resignation.*

**8.** (1) A member may, by writing under his hand addressed to the Minister, resign from the Committee.

(2) Where a member, without the leave of the Minister, absents himself from three consecutive quarterly meetings of the Committee, he shall be deemed to have resigned.

*Revocation of appointment to the Committee.*

**9.** The Minister may, at any time, revoke the appointment of a member of the Committee.

*Filing vacancies in the Committee.*

**10.** (1) Where a vacancy in the Committee is occasioned by death, resignation or other cause, the Minister may, subject to section 8 (1) of the Act appoint another person to be a member of the Committee for the remainder of its term.

(2) The Committee may act notwithstanding any

vacancy among the members thereof.

*Appointment of Secretary to the Committee.*

**11.** (1) The Committee may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint some fit and proper person to be Secretary of the Committee at such salary as is approved by the Minister.

(2) The Secretary shall not be dismissed without the approval of the Minister.

*Duties of Secretary.*

**12.** (1) The Secretary shall perform such duties as shall be assigned to him by the Committee, and shall on all occasions in the execution of his office act under the superintendence, control and direction of the Committee.

(2) The Secretary shall attend all meetings of the Committee and record correctly the minutes of the proceedings thereof.

*Meetings of the Committee.*

**13.** (1) The Committee shall meet at least once in every three months on such date as may be fixed by the Committee.

(2) The Committee shall also meet as often as it may be summoned either by direction of the Chairman or on the written requisition of at least four members of the Committee who shall sign the same. In such requisition there shall be stated the nature of the business to be transacted at the meeting and the requisition shall be delivered to the Secretary.

(3) The Secretary shall summon a meeting of the Committee on, and in accordance with, the direction of the Chairman, and also within three days after the receipt by him of a requisition made in accordance with paragraph (2).

(4) Every notice convening a meeting of the Committee shall state the time and place of the meeting and

the nature of the business to be transacted at such meeting.

*Travelling and subsistence expenses.*

**14.** The reasonable travelling and subsistence expenses incurred by members of the Committee in attending meetings of the Committee, or while engaged on any business of the Committee, shall be paid by the Committee.
