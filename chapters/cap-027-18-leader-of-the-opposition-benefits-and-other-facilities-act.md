*6 of 2010*

An Act to provide for benefits and other facilities to the Leader of the Opposition.

### [9 TH JUNE, 2010]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Leader of the Opposition (Benefits and Other Facilities) Act.

*Benefits and certain other facilities of the Leader of the Opposition.*

**2.** The Leader of the Opposition shall be entitled at Government expense to the following—

(a) a rent free furnished office accommodation;

(b) medical attention including medical treatment or reimbursement of medical expenses incurred by him for himself and the dependant members of his family;

(c) full-time security service at his official place of residence and at the office of the Leader of the Opposition to be

provided by the Guyana Police Force;

(d) the services of a research assistant, an executive assistant/secretary, a clerical/office assistant, a chauffeur, a personal security officer, a gardener and two domestic servants;

(e) salary, vacation allowance and parliamentary benefits equivalent to those of a Cabinet Minister.

*Regulations.*

**3.** (1) The Minister responsible for finance may make regulations for carrying out the provisions of this Act.

(2) Every regulation made under this Act shall be laid as soon as possible after it is made before the National Assembly.
