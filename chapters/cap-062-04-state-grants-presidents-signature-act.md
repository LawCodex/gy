*42 of 1930*

An Act to authorise the President’s Signature to State Grants and Leases to be attached by a Stamp.

[31 st DECEMBER, 1930]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the State Grants (President’s Signature) Act.

*President’s*

**2.** It shall be lawful for the President in all State signature may grants and leases of land hereafter to be made by him, to be attached by

*stamp or facsimile.*

cause his signature thereto to be attached by means of a stamp or facsimile of his sign manual, and grants and leases so signed shall be of the same effect, force, and validity to all intents and purposes as if the said grants and leases bore the actual autograph or sign manual of the President:

Provided always that all such grants and leases shall be countersigned by the Commissioner of Lands or other person duly appointed by the President in that behalf.

*President’s signature to State grants and leases when attached By stamp to be attested by Commissioner of lands or other officer.*

**3.** Whenever the President shall, by virtue of the powers conferred upon him by section 2, cause his signature to be attached to any State grant or lease of land by means of a stamp or facsimile of his sign manual, every such signature shall be so attached in the presence of the Commissioner of Lands, or of some other officer duly authorised by the President on that behalf, who shall certify on the face of such State grant or lease that the President’s signature was so attached in his presence.

*Offences.*

**4.** Every person—

(a) who shall without authority of the President obtain possession of, or who shall without authority of the President affix, the stamp or facsimile of the sign manual of the President to any document purporting to be a State grant or lease of land; or

(b) who shall forge, counterfeit, or cause or procure to be forged or counterfeited, any die, plate, or other instrument or any part of any die, plate, or other instrument which has been or shall or may be provided, made, or used by or under the direction of the President (or by or under the direction of any person or persons legally authorised in that behalf) for the purpose of attaching his signature to State grants or leases in manner aforesaid; or

(c) who shall forge, counterfeit, or imitate, or cause or procure to be forged, counterfeited, or imitated, the
