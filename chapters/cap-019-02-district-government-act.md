An Act to make provision for the better Government of the Outlying Parts of Guyana.

### [7 TH JANUARY,1911]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the District Government Act.

## PART I PROCLAMATION OF DISTRICTS

*Establishment of administrative districts.*

**2.** (1) The Minister may by order declare and establish in any part of Guyana a district (hereinafter described as “the district”) to be administered in the manner hereinafter mentioned.

(2) The order shall set forth the boundaries of the

district.

(3) The Minister may from time to time, by order, extend, or reduce, or otherwise alter, the limits of the district.

*North Western district.*

**3.** The north western district declared and established under the (repealed) North Western District Government Ordinance, 1890, shall (repealed) North Western District Government Ordinance, 1890.
