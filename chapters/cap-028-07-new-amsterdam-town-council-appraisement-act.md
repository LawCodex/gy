*37 of 1974*

An Act to provide for the making of appraisements in the Town of New Amsterdam pursuant to the continuance in operation of certain provisions of the New Amsterdam Town Council Ordinance.

### [7 TH NOVEMBER, 1974]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the New Amsterdam Town Council (Appraisement) Act.

*Order to enable the making of appraisements of properties in New Amsterdam deemed to have been made.*

**2.** Notwithstanding any law to the contrary, an order under section 136 of the New Amsterdam Town Council Ordinance continued in force by section 44 of the Valuation for Rating Purposes Act) for the purpose of extending to the 31st December, 1975 the period within which the New Amsterdam Town Council shall order and direct the general appraisement of all properties in the Town of New Amsterdam shall for all purposes be deemed to have been

Cap. 161 duly made and to have come into operation on 1st January, 1953 Ed.

1974.
