*8 of 1946*

An Act to provide for the Preservation and Maintenance of the Navigation Markers for the Demerara River and for purposes connected with matters aforesaid.

### [20 TH MAY, 1946]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Demerara River Navigation Markers Act.

Interpretation. 2. In this Act-

“navigation marker” means a structure carrying a light or other mark –

(a) bearing the number respectively

*Schedule.*

specified in the first column of the Schedule;

(b) erected under the authority of the Defence Regulations, 1939, on lands on the banks of the Demerara River; and

*Schedule.*

(c) situated as described in the second column of the Schedule.

*Vesting in the State of*

**3.** As from the 9th May, 1945, there shall be vested in

*the State – navigation markers and of*

*rights incidental thereto.*

(a) the navigation markers under this Act;

(b) the right to occupy and use the sites of the aforesaid navigation markers for the purpose of keeping such markers on such sites, and of maintaining the markers in working order;

*Schedule.*

(c) a right of way over each and every of the lands (of which the sites as aforesaid respectively form parts) described in the second column of the Schedule for the purpose of obtaining ingress, from the nearest practicable point on the Demerara River or on the public road, to the respective sites of the navigation markers as aforesaid, and for the purpose of egress from such sites to the Demerara River or the public road, as the case may be.

*Right of way.*

**4.** The right of way under section 3 shall be a way of necessity, and shall be exercised by the agents and servants of

the Government and by any person authorised in writing by the Commissioner of Lands so to do, and in the exercise of such right –

(a) a path leading to any site as aforesaid may be cut and kept open for the purposes of this Act;

*Schedule.*

(b) no undue interference or disturbance shall be caused to the lands set out in the second column of the Schedule.

*Servicing of lights.*

**5.** It shall be the duty of Sprostons Limited, to service the lights associated with the navigation markers as aforesaid, and Sprostons Limited, by its agents, servants and workmen shall from time to time have power, for such purpose, to exercise the right of way under section 3, to go to and return from the sites of the navigation markers as aforesaid.

*Compensation.*

*c. 62:05*

**6.** Compensation shall be assessed, and paid by the Government in respect of the vesting of the rights under section 3 in the same manner and to the same extent as if such rights were compulsorily acquired by the State under section 9 of the Acquisition of Lands for Public Purposes Act.

*Offences. [6 of 1997]*

**7.** Any person who –

(a) unlawfully damages, injures, tampers with, removes or destroys a navigation marker under this Act, or any light used in connection therewith; or

(b) wilfully obstructs the exercise of a right of way under this Act.

shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of thirteen thousand dollars.

*Where the navigation marker no longer required and where the situation of marker altered.*

**8.** The Minister may from time to time by order –

(a) declare that the site on which any navigation marker is erected is no longer required for the purposes of this Act, and thereupon this Act shall cease to apply to such navigation marker and to the rights of occupation, user and way relating to such marker;

(b) vary the situation of any navigation marker, and thereupon this Act shall apply to the navigation marker in the same manner and to the like extent as if the situation, as varied, was described in the second column of the Schedule.
