## SCHEDULE—Transfer of War Grave.

*1929 Ed. c. 252 1953 Ed. c. 356 _______________________________________________________*

*24 of 1928 An*

Act to make provision for granting to the Commonwealth War Graves Commission the exclusive right and title to the graves of members of the Armed Forces of the British Crown who died in the Great War.

[ 22 nd DECEMBER, 1928]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the War Graves Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act—

*[29 of 1961]*

“the Commission” means the Commonwealth War Graves Commission;

“the great war” means the European War of 1914 to August 31st, 1921;

“owner” means a person having a title to land by grant from the State or by transport;

“war grave” means the grave of any member of the armed forces of the British Crown who died from wounds inflicted, accident occurring, or disease contracted, while on active service whether on sea or land, during the great war.

*Transfer of title to grave.*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of any Act, the owner of any land in which there is a war grave may cede and transfer to the Commission all his right and title to a specified portion of the land containing the grave, to be held by the Commission or their successors in perpetuity.

*Schedule.*

(2) The transfer shall be in the form set out in the Schedule, and shall have the effect of vesting in the Commission the same right and title as would be vested by a transport to the like effect passed and executed before a judge.

*Registration of title.*

**4.** The person who transfers his right and title to land as provided in the last preceding section shall certify to the Commissioner of Lands or to the Registrar of Deeds, according as the land is held by grant from the State or by transport, the particulars of the land, and the Commissioner or the Registrar shall thereupon annotate the grant or transport respectively of any land affected by the transfer.

*Savings consequent on change of name. [29 of 1961]*

**5.** (1) The change of name of the Commission (from the Imperial War Graves Commission to the Commonwealth War Graves Commission) effected by the Miscellaneous Enactments (Amendment) Ordinance, 1961, shall in no way prejudice or affect any rights or obligations of the Commission or anything which remains to be done or forborne to be done under or by virtue of its previous name, and the Commission may exercise by its new name all

existing rights, powers and privileges.

(2) Any reference to the Commission by its previous name in any enactment, instrument or document of any kind shall be construed as a reference to the Commission by its new name.
