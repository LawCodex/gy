## SCHEDULE—Salaries prescribed

__________________________

26 of 1962 An Act to prescribe the salaries and allowances payable to the holders of certain offices under the Constitution.

### [31ST DECEMBER, 1962]

Short title.

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Constitutional Offices

[O. 38/1966A] (Remuneration of Holders) Act.

*Salaries*

**2.** (1) The amounts specified in the Schedule in prescribed. relation to the persons holding the offices specified therein, Schedule. are hereby prescribed as the salaries payable annually to the said persons.

(2) The amounts aforesaid shall grow due and become payable monthly on the last day of each month in the year:

Provided that—

(a) the amounts so to be paid for or in respect of each month shall not exceed the aggregate amount of all such salaries for the month, the monthly salary of a person holding an office aforesaid being one-twelfth of his annual salary;

(b) where a person is appointed to be a member of more than one Service Commission, he shall only be entitled to be paid salary as prescribed in respect of his membership of one Service Commission, and where the amount of salary prescribed for members of the respective Service Commissions is not the same, the said person shall be entitled to be paid whichever amount of salary is greater or greatest, as the case may be.

(3) In this section, the expression “Service Commission” means the Judicial Service Commission or the Public Service Commission or the Police Service Commission established under the Constitution.

*Allowances. Schedule.*

**3.** There shall be paid to the holder of any office specified in the Schedule such allowances as may from time to time be prescribed by order of the Minister responsible for finance (hereinafter referred to as “the Minister”).

*Exemption from customs duties of goods imported by the President.*

**4.** No customs duties or bill of entry tax shall be charged, levied or paid on goods imported by or on behalf of the President for his personal use.

*Power to amend*

**5.** Subject to negative resolution of the National

*Schedule. O. 38/1966A.*

Assembly, the Minister may from time to time, by order, amend the provisions of the Schedule.

*Retrospective operation of orders made under section 3 and 5.*

**6.** Any order made under section 3 or 5 may contain provisions authorising the payment of the allowance or salary from a date earlier than the date on which the order is published in the Gazette, and such earlier date may be a date prior to the commencement of this Act.
