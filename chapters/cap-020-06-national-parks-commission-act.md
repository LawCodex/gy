*Establishment and constitution*

**3.** (1) There is hereby established a body to be

*of the National Parks Commission.*

known as the National Parks Commission which shall be a body corporate.

(2) The Commission shall consist of not less than four nor more than eleven members appointed by the Minister.

(3) Any member other than the Chairman or Deputy Chairman may at any time resign his office as such by instrument in writing addressed to the Chairman who shall cause the same to be forwarded to the Minister and upon the date of the receipt by the Chairman of the instrument such member shall cease to be a member.

(4) The Chairman or Deputy Chairman may at any time resign his office as such by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and such resignation shall take effect upon the date of the receipt of such instrument by the Minister.

(5) If the Chairman or Deputy Chairman ceases to be a member, he shall also cease to be Chairman or Deputy Chairman as the case may be.

(6) Four members of the Commission shall form a quorum.

(7) There shall be paid to the Chairman and other members of the Commission such remuneration, if any (whether by way of salaries or travelling or other allowances), as the Minister may determine.

(8) The appointment, removal from office, or cessation of the tenure of office, of the Chairman or any other member shall be notified in the Gazette.

*\5. It*

*Duties and functions of the Commission.*

shall be the function of the Commission to—

(a) maintain and regulate the use of any designated park so as to leave it unimpaired for the enjoyment of future generations;

(b) make recommendations to the National Trust to achieve the conservation of the natural beauty of any area of land in Guyana and to carry out such acts as the National Trust directs for the purpose of attaining the objectives of those recommendations;

(c) collaborate with local government authorities in developing any area of land subject to the control of any such authority for the purpose of affording to members of the public opportunity for open air recreations;

(d) provide to local government authorities maintenance services in respect of parks on such terms and conditions as the Commission sees fit;

(e) give general advice to the Minister or any statutory body in relation to the provision or improvement of opportunities for the enjoyment of the countryside by members of the public.

*Remuneration and appointment of employees.*

**7.** (1) The Commission may appoint and employ upon such terms and conditions as it thinks fit, such employees and agents as the Commission deems necessary for the proper carrying out of the provisions of this Act:

Provided that—

(c) the Commission shall not terminate, without the prior approval of the Minister, the employment of any person who holds a post the salary of which is in excess of three thousand dollars per annum.

(2) The Commission may, at any time, retain the services of professional persons and may pay such remuneration in respect thereof as the Commission, with the approval of the Minister, may determine.

*Protection of members of the Commission.*

**8.** (1) No action, suit, prosecution or other proceedings shall be brought or instituted personally against any member in respect of any act done bona fide in pursuance of the execution or intended execution of this Act.

(2) Where any member is exempt from liability by reason only of the provisions of subsection (1), the Commission shall be liable to the extent that it would be if such member were an officer or other employee, or agent, of the Commission.

*Accounts and reports to the Minister.*

**12.** (1) The accounts of the Commission shall be audited annually by the Director of Audit and the members, officers and employees of the Commission shall grant to the Director of Audit access to all the books, documents, cash and securities of the Commission and shall give to him on request all such information as shall be within their knowledge in relation to the operation of the Commission.

*By-laws. [6 of 1997]*

**13.** (1) The Commission may, with the approval of the Minister, make by-laws in respect of any or all designated parks for the purpose of carrying out the functions of the Commission under this Act, and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, such by-laws may—

(a) make provision for the imposition and collection of fees under which any person may enter, travel through, or in any way make use of such parks and any of the facilities therein;

(b) prohibit or regulate the use of the park or any part thereof either generally or in any manner specified in the by-laws, by traffic of any description so specified;

(c) contain provisions prohibiting the depositing of rubbish and the leaving of litter;

(d) prohibit or restrict the killing, taking, molesting or disturbance of living

creatures of any description in a designated park, the taking, destruction or disturbance of eggs of any such creature, the taking of, or interference with, vegetation of any description in such park or the doing of anything therein which will interfere with the soil or damage any object in the park.

(2) By-laws made under subsection (1) may provide that the breach thereof shall be an offence punishable on summary conviction by a fine not exceeding twenty-seven thousand, five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for six months.

(3) By-laws may provide for the recovery by the Commission from any offender, in addition to any penalty imposed under subsection (2), or from the proceeds of any sale by the Commission of property taken possession of by the Commission in breach of such by-laws, of the expenses incurred by the Commission in consequence of such breach and the exercise by the Commission of its powers to ensure compliance with the by-laws.

(4) At least fourteen days before application for the approval of the Minister of the by-laws is made, notice of the intention to apply for approval shall be published and a copy of the proposed by-laws for which approval is intended to be sought shall be deposited in the office of the Commission and be open for public inspection without payment of fee at all reasonable hours during the period ensuing between the publication and the day of application.

(5) All by-laws submitted to the Minister for approval shall be accompanied by copies of any objection to the adoption of the by-laws which has been lodged in writing with the Chairman or if no such objection has been

lodged, a certificate by the Chairman to that effect.

(6) The Minister may approve with or without amendment or reject any by-laws submitted to him.

(7) A copy of by-laws which have been approved shall be deposited at the office of the Commission and be open at all reasonable hours to public inspection without fee and another copy shall be kept displayed at a conspicuous place in the park to which the by-laws relate.

*Exemption from taxes.*

**14.** The Commission shall be exempt from the payment of any licence fees, duties of customs, purchase tax and income tax, in respect of any goods bought for the use or income accruing to the Commission, as the case may be.

*Statutory Authorities and the Commission.*

**15.** (1) Nothing in this Act shall be deemed to confer on the Commission any function conferred by law on any other authority and the Commission in the carrying out of the functions conferred upon it by this Act shall do so in a manner as would facilitate the carrying out by that other authority of its functions.

(2) Subject to section 14 and to subsection (3) of this section, the provisions of this Act shall not derogate from the requirements of any other enactment providing for the exercise of the functions of any authority established under the Town and Country Planning Act, and enactments relating to public health, public utilities and the control of road traffic.

(3) An authority referred to in subsection (2) shall—

(a) consult with the Commission whenever such authority considers that any proposed work of construction would hinder or otherwise adversely

affect the conservation of the natural beauty of an area;

(b) in the carrying out of its functions consider any recommendations made by the Commission in relation to measures which the Commission considers would promote the conservation of the natural beauty of an area.

*Citation.*

**1.** These By-laws may be cited as the National Parks

*By-laws.*

*\2. The Hours of*

*opening of the 5.30 o’ clock National Park. afternoon of*

National Park shall be open to the public from in the forenoon until 7.30 o’ clock in the every day:

*Provided*

that –

(a) the National Park may, with the permission of the Commission, be open during any other period for the holding of public events;

(b) if the Commission is satisfied that the right of public access to the Park will not unreasonably interfere with, it may, on giving at least seven days’ notice in a daily newspaper circulating in Guyana and in such conspicuous places in the National

Park as it thinks fit for the purpose, authorize the use of the Park by any organization or other body of persons for the purposes of the organization or body of persons and the closure of the Park to the activities normally carried on therein for such period as may be determined by the commission.

*Trespassers.*

**3.** No person shall, without lawful excuse (the proof whereof shall lie on him), be in the National Park except during any period when the Park is open to the public in accordance with by-law 2.

*Entry and exit.*

**4.** No person shall enter or leave the National Park except through any gate ordinary used by the public.

*Traffic signs.*

**5.** Every person in the National Park shall comply with the traffic signs installed in the Park by the Commission.

*Hunting shooting or fishing in National Park prohibited.*

**6.** No person shall engage in hunting, shooting, fishing (otherwise then by the use of a rod), snaring, or the taking or destroying of animals, birds or fish in the National Park, or the taking destroying or disturbance of the eggs of any bird or fish therein.

*Playing on or using fields when wet prohibited.*

**7.** No person shall play on or use the fields of the National Park when they are in a wet or soggy condition.

*Permission to hold public meeting.*

**8.** No person shall hold any public meeting in the National Park unless the permission in writing of the commission has been first obtained.

*Obstructing or hindering.*

**9.** No person shall hinder or obstruct any officer or other employee of the Commission or any police constable in the exercise of any of his functions in respect of the National

National Parks By-Laws

Park.

*Commission may impose conditions for use of the National Park.*

**10.** (1) Where the Commission permits the National Park to be used for any purpose by any organisation or other body of persons, the Commission may impose such terms and conditions as it thinks fit for the use of the Park.

(2) Where any term or condition imposed by the Commission is not complied with, the Commission may take such measures as it thinks fit (including the pre-emptory withdrawal of any permission granted for the use of the National Park or the taking of control of any gate providing entry to, or exit from the Park by any of its officers or other employees with the assistance of any police constable) for the purpose of securing compliance with any such term or condition.

*Penalty.*

**11.** Any person who –

(a) contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of these by-laws or

(b) breaches any term or condition imposed by the Commission pursuant to by law 10.

shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for a term of six months without prejudice, however, to the exercise by the Commission of its powers pursuant to by-law 10 (2).
