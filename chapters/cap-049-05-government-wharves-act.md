*5 of 1903*

An Act to make provision for the regulation of Government Wharves.

### [4 TH MARCH, 1903]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Government Wharves Act.

*Interpretation. [4 of 1972] Schedule.*

**2.** In this Act “government wharf” means any wharf mentioned in the Schedule.

*Minister may declare Government wharves. [49 of 1952 4 of 1972]*

**3.** The Minister may from time to time, by notice published in the Gazette amend the Schedule by adding any Wharf thereto or by deleting therefrom any existing Wharf.

*Power to Minister to make regulations.*

**4.** Subject to negative resolution of the National Assembly, the Minister may make regulations for all or any of the matters following:

(a) (i) the persons who shall be permitted to use, (ii) the vessels which may be permitted to use, (iii) the manner in which vessels shall approach, lie alongside, and depart from, (iv) the manner in which passengers shall land and embark at, (v) the manner in which cargo may be loaded and discharged at, (vi) the tolls or other payments to be made by any person or vessel using,

any government wharf;

(b) the safety and preservation of the structure of any government wharf, and the prevention of collisions, and other accidents on it, or in its vicinity; and

(c) generally, to provide for the management, control, and regulation of government wharves, and for the safety and convenience of persons and vessels using them.

*Enforcement. [7 of 1998]*

**4A.** (1) Notwithstanding any provision in this Act and in any subsidiary legislation made thereunder, it shall be the duty of the Director and the Maritime Administration Department to enforce or cause to be enforced the provisions

thereof and a reference therein to an officer or body shall accordingly be construed as a reference to the “Director” and the “Maritime Administration Department”, respectively.

*c. 49:01*

(2) In this Act the words “Director” and “Maritime Administration Department” have the same meaning assigned to those words in sections 2 and 4, respectively of the Guyana Shipping Act.

### TRAFFIC ON GOVERNMENT WHARVES REGULATIONS

made under section 4

*Citation.*

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Traffic on Government Wharves Regulations.

*Use of certain wharves.*

**2.** No vessel or craft except the steamer or craft of the Government shall be allowed to use the Government Steamer Wharf, Demerara, or East or West ferry wharves, Demerara River.

*Permission required.*

**3.** No vessel or craft shall be permitted to use any Government wharf at which Government Steamers call, unless permission shall have been previously obtained from the Management of the Government Steamers, except in the case of accidents, loss of life, or perils of the sea.

*Cargo.*

**4.** All cargo while lying on the wharf shall be so packed as to cause no obstruction to traffic.

*Securing of vessels etc.*

**5.** No vessel or craft shall be made fast to the handrails or any part of a wharf except the bollards provided for that purpose.

*Wharf to be cleaned.*

**6.** Any person using a wharf for the purpose of shipping or landing cargo or animals shall properly clean the wharf after so using it.

*Penalties. [4 of 1972]*

**7.** Any person committing a breach of any of these regulations, shall be liable to a fine of seventy-five dollars.

*Reg. 5/1962 [4 of 1972]*

THE GOVERNMENT WHARVES (PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT) REGULATIONS.

made under section 4

*Citation.*

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Government Wharves (Public Works Department) Regulations.

*Permission.*

**2.** Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary contained in any law, no vessel or craft shall be permitted to use any government wharf, under the control of the Public Works Department, unless permission shall have been previously obtained from the Chief Works and Hydraulics Officer or his duly authorised officer, except in the case of accidents, loss of life or perils at sea. Any permission granted in accordance with these Rules shall be subject to the user giving way to vessels and craft of the Public Works Department.

*Cargo.*

**3.** All cargo lying on any such wharf or its approaches shall be so packed as to cause no obstruction to traffic or to cause no damage to the superstructure or substructure of the said wharf.

*Securing vessels etc.*

**4.** No vessel or craft shall be made fast to any part of any such wharf except to the bollards provided for that purpose.

*Discharging cargo.*

**5.** No vessel shall lie alongside at or discharge cargo on any portion of any such wharf other than that appointed by the Chief Works and Hydraulics Officer or his duly authorised representative.

*Removal of cargo.*

**6.** All cargo discharged on any such wharf shall be removed immediately upon request of the Chief Works and Hydraulics Officer or his duly authorised representative by the person to whom such cargo is consigned or his agent.
